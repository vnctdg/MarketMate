# MarketMate

**MarketMate** is a mobile application designed to make shopping in local markets easier, smarter, and more convenient. It connects market vendors and buyers on a digital platform, allowing users to browse products, compare prices, and place orders directly from their mobile devices.

---

## Key Features

- **Vendor Listings**  
  Vendors can showcase their available products, prices, and daily promotions.

- **Smart Search & Filter**  
  Users can search for specific items and filter by price, freshness, or vendor rating.

- **Digital Cart & Checkout**  
  Buyers can add items from multiple vendors into a single cart for seamless checkout.

- **Real-time Updates**  
  Vendors can instantly update product availability, ensuring accurate listings.

- **Secure Payment Options**  
  Offers cash on delivery and digital wallet integration for secure transactions.
